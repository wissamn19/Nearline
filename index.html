How it works

1

Type

One bar, no menus and no folders. Press / anywhere to jump to it, then Enter.

2

It files itself

Links match a rule, a leading ! makes a note, and a small model decides the rest. A tag shows where it will land.

3

It reminds you

Add @17:30 to anything. At that time a card appears with Done and Snooze 10 min.

Everything on one road

Your entries live on a roadmap with three stops. New items drive along the road to their stop.

+

Click a stop to choose

Disagree with the sorting? Click Task, Note or Link and type straight into it.

⇅

Fix mistakes in one click

Every card has a Move to menu. Corrections are saved as training data.

✓

Progress at a glance

The Task stop shows how many are done, like 3/5, and ticking a box updates it.

●

Private by design

No account and no server. Everything is saved in your browser, on your device.

↓

Your data, your file

Type /export to download your entries as a CSV.

☾

Light and dark

It follows your system theme, and it respects reduced motion.

A small model, honestly measured

Telling a task from a note is the hard part. Nearline uses a tiny model trained on hand-labeled examples. It runs in your browser, with no server and no API.

~72%

right on task versus note, measured with cross-validation on 124 examples

1 click

to fix a wrong sort, and every fix is collected to retrain the model

0 servers

the model is 2048 numbers in a file, and your text never leaves the page

Links are never guessed: a rule files them. And the ! prefix always wins over the model.
